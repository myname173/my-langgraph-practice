# Lighting Grammar — Cinematography Knowledge Base

Structured lighting vocabulary for AI-generated game trailers.
Each entry maps a lighting technique to its visual effect and genre association.

---

## Key Lighting Setups

| Technique | Visual Effect | Genre / Mood |
|---|---|---|
| three-point lighting | balanced, dimensional subject | standard cinematic, neutral |
| Rembrandt lighting | triangle shadow on cheek | dramatic, portrait, serious |
| split lighting | half face lit, half in shadow | duality, conflict, mystery |
| butterfly lighting | shadow under nose, symmetrical | glamour, beauty, elegance |
| loop lighting | small shadow to one side of nose | natural, approachable |

## Atmospheric Lighting

| Technique | Visual Effect | Genre / Mood |
|---|---|---|
| volumetric fog | light rays visible in haze | depth, mystery, fantasy |
| god rays | shafts of light through gaps | divine, hopeful, cathedral |
| rain reflection | wet surface light bounce | realism, noir, melancholy |
| dust particles in light | floating motes in beam | age, abandonment, atmosphere |
| haze / smoke | soft diffusion of light | tension, battlefield, industrial |

## Stylized Lighting

| Technique | Visual Effect | Genre / Mood |
|---|---|---|
| neon rim light | colored edge glow on subject | cyberpunk, sci-fi, futuristic |
| bloom highlight | overexposed glow around bright sources | cinematic intensity, dreamlike |
| chromatic aberration light | color fringing on bright edges | glitch, digital, unstable |
| flickering light | intermittent illumination | horror, tension, malfunction |
| color gel lighting | saturated colored light on subject | stylized, mood-driven, artistic |

## Prompt Keywords for Image Generation

When writing lighting prompts for AI image generation models, combine these descriptors:

- `cinematic lighting` — general high-quality film lighting
- `volumetric fog, god rays` — atmospheric depth
- `neon rim light, cyberpunk aesthetic` — sci-fi edge glow
- `backlight silhouette, hero framing` — iconic subject outline
- `rain-soaked streets, reflection lighting` — wet surface realism
- `bloom highlight, lens flare` — cinematic intensity
- `low-key lighting, deep shadows` — noir, mystery
- `high-key lighting, minimal shadows` — bright, clean, optimistic
- `practical lighting only` — motivated by in-scene light sources
- `subsurface scattering on skin` — realistic skin translucency
