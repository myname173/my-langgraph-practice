# Trailer Patterns — Narrative Structure Knowledge Base

Proven narrative templates for game trailer construction.
Each pattern defines a sequence of beats that form a compelling trailer arc.

---

## Core Trailer Arc (Universal)

Every effective game trailer follows this 4-beat structure:

1. **World Building** — Establish the setting, tone, and atmosphere
2. **Character Introduction** — Reveal the protagonist and their world
3. **Conflict Escalation** — Raise stakes through action and tension
4. **Emotional Silence Ending** — A quiet, resonant final moment

---

## Pattern: Epic Action Trailer

Best for: AAA action, hack-and-slash, open-world games

| Beat | Duration | Shot Type | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. World reveal | 5-8s | extreme wide → wide | landscape, city, battlefield establishing scale |
| 2. Hero entrance | 5-8s | medium → close-up | protagonist revealed, signature pose or weapon |
| 3. Action montage | 8-12s | tracking + handheld | combat, abilities, enemy encounters |
| 4. Climax moment | 3-5s | close-up → extreme close-up | signature move, boss reveal, or power moment |
| 5. Silence beat | 2-3s | wide or close-up | stillness before title card |

Camera tendency: low angle hero shots, orbit reveals, crane ascents
Lighting tendency: volumetric fog, rim light, high contrast

---

## Pattern: Mystery / Horror Trailer

Best for: horror, thriller, narrative puzzle games

| Beat | Duration | Shot Type | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Uneasy calm | 5-8s | wide, static | ordinary environment with subtle wrongness |
| 2. Discovery | 5-8s | dolly in → close-up | character finds clue, hears sound, senses presence |
| 3. Escalation | 8-12s | handheld, dutch angle | tension builds, glimpses of threat |
| 4. Jump / reveal | 2-3s | extreme close-up | flash of horror, then cut to black |
| 5. Title in silence | 2-3s | black screen | title card with ambient sound only |

Camera tendency: dolly in, dutch angle, handheld instability
Lighting tendency: low-key, flickering, split lighting, deep shadows

---

## Pattern: Emotional Story Trailer

Best for: RPG, narrative adventure, story-driven games

| Beat | Duration | Shot Type | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Peaceful world | 5-8s | wide, golden hour | beautiful environment in calm state |
| 2. Character bond | 5-8s | medium, close-up | protagonist with companion, showing connection |
| 3. Loss / threat | 8-12s | mixed, escalating | something disrupts the peace, stakes revealed |
| 4. Resolve | 3-5s | close-up, low angle | character decides to fight, emotional determination |
| 5. Quiet ending | 3-5s | wide, pulling away | character walks toward destiny, bittersweet |

Camera tendency: crane movements, dolly out, steadicam follow
Lighting tendency: golden hour transition to blue hour, volumetric atmosphere

---

## Pattern: Competitive / Esports Trailer

Best for: fighting, shooter, multiplayer competitive games

| Beat | Duration | Shot Type | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Arena reveal | 3-5s | wide, dynamic | stage or map establishing shot |
| 2. Character roster | 8-12s | orbit + close-up montage | rapid character showcases, signature moves |
| 3. Combat highlight | 8-12s | tracking, whip pan | fast-paced gameplay action |
| 4. Ultimate moment | 3-5s | slow-mo close-up | super move, headshot, or finishing blow |
| 5. Logo slam | 2-3s | — | title with impact sound |

Camera tendency: whip pans, orbit shots, fast tracking
Lighting tendency: neon, bloom highlights, high saturation

## Pacing Guidelines

| Trailer Length | Recommended Shots | Rhythm |
|---|---|---|
| 15 seconds | 3-4 shots | fast cuts, high energy throughout |
| 30 seconds | 4-6 shots | build from medium to fast pace |
| 60 seconds | 6-10 shots | slow start → accelerate → silence ending |
| 90+ seconds | 10-15 shots | full arc with breathing room between peaks |

## Transition Techniques

- **Hard cut**: instant switch, maintains energy
- **Match cut**: similar composition, different subject — creates visual rhyme
- **Whip pan**: fast blur transition — suggests speed and energy
- **Fade to black**: time passage, dramatic pause
- **Smash cut**: quiet → loud contrast — shock and surprise
- **Parallel cut**: alternate between two threads — builds tension toward convergence
