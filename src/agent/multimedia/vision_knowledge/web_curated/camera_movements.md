# AI 视频运镜提示词宝典

> 来源：CSDN、Digitaling、GitHub ai-shortfilm-prompts 等高质量技术社区，经筛选整理。
> 用途：AI 视频生成（可灵 / Sora / Wan / Kling / Runway 等）的运镜提示词参考。

---

## 万能运镜公式

构建高质量运镜提示词的六层结构：

**景别设定** + **摄影机轨迹** + **运动速率** + **核心行为** + **场景细节** + **光影质感**

示例：Medium shot, slow and steady camera push-in. A girl looks up at the sky on a rainy night street. Raindrops on her cheeks, neon reflecting on wet pavement, cinematic lighting.

---

## 情感递进推轨 (Emotional Push-in / Dolly In)

靠近主体，强化情感与细节。

**中文 Prompt：**
镜头从远景开始，摄像机缓慢向前推进，逐渐靠近站在古桥上的年轻剑客。画面从全景过渡到中景，最终停留在面部特写。雨水划过脸颊，远处灯火在雨幕中晕染开来，电影级情绪光影。

**English Prompt：**
Starting wide, the camera slowly pushes in on a young swordsman standing on an ancient bridge. Transitioning from full shot to close-up, rain slides down his face while distant lanterns blur through the rain curtain, cinematic emotional lighting.

**核心关键词：** dolly in, push in, emotional intensification, narrowing focus

---

## 真相揭示拉远 (Truth Reveal Pull-out / Dolly Out)

远离主体，揭示环境与空间。

**中文 Prompt：**
镜头从泛黄照片特写开始，摄像机缓慢向后拉远。逐渐露出女孩身影，最终展现被废弃的未来城市。残破高楼与荒草覆盖街道形成强烈反差。

**English Prompt：**
Starting with a close-up of a yellowed photograph, the camera slowly pulls back to reveal a girl's silhouette, then an abandoned futuristic city. Crumbling skyscrapers and overgrown streets create stark contrast.

**核心关键词：** dolly out, pull back, revealing context, expanding perspective

---

## 视线平移探索 (Panning Exploration)

水平扫视，揭示广阔场景。

**中文 Prompt：**
镜头从山谷左侧缓慢移动至右侧。画面依次经过瀑布、森林和未来城市。飞行器穿过晨雾，阳光从云层间洒落。

**English Prompt：**
The camera pans slowly left to right across a valley, passing waterfalls, ancient forests, and a futuristic city. Aircraft pierce the morning mist as sunlight filters through cloud layers.

**核心关键词：** pan left/right, horizontal sweep, landscape exploration

---

## 侧面平行追踪 (Parallel Tracking Shot)

跟随主体平行移动，增强速度感。

**中文 Prompt：**
摄像机从侧面同步跟随高速骑行的机车骑士。主体始终位于画面中央，背景霓虹灯形成横向光轨，展现极强速度感。

**English Prompt：**
The camera tracks synchronously from the side with a speeding motorcyclist. The subject stays centered while neon backgrounds form horizontal light trails, conveying intense velocity.

**核心关键词：** tracking shot, lateral movement, follow shot, parallel motion

---

## 背后沉浸跟随 (Immersive Follow Shot)

第三人称追随视角，增强参与感。

**中文 Prompt：**
摄像机位于主角身后持续跟拍。主角快速穿过拥挤市场，两侧摊位和人群不断后退，形成强烈沉浸式追逐体验。

**English Prompt：**
The camera continuously follows behind the protagonist running through a crowded market. Stalls and people rush backward on both sides, creating an immersive chase experience.

**核心关键词：** follow shot, behind subject, immersive pursuit, Steadicam feel

---

## 垂直空间升维 (Vertical Crane Ascension)

垂直升高，营造宏伟感。

**中文 Prompt：**
镜头从广场中央开始缓慢升空。随着高度不断增加，整座未来都市逐渐展现在画面中，巨型建筑群延伸至地平线。

**English Prompt：**
The camera slowly ascends from the center of a plaza. As altitude increases, an entire futuristic metropolis unfolds, with massive architectural clusters stretching to the horizon.

**核心关键词：** crane shot, vertical movement, sweeping ascent, grandeur

---

## 360度英雄环绕 (360 Hero Orbit Shot)

圆周运动，强调空间纵深和史诗感。

**中文 Prompt：**
摄像机围绕角色进行360度平滑环绕。能量粒子围绕身体流动，背景光效不断变化，营造主角登场的史诗感。

**English Prompt：**
The camera smoothly orbits the character in a full 360-degree arc. Energy particles flow around the body with constantly shifting background lighting, creating an epic hero entrance moment.

**核心关键词：** orbit shot, 360-degree, arc shot, hero reveal, spatial depth

---

## 希区柯克变焦 (Dolly Zoom / Vertigo Effect)

推拉结合扭曲背景，表现眩晕震惊。

**中文 Prompt：**
摄像机缓慢后退，同时镜头持续变焦推进。角色始终保持相同大小，背景山谷被不断拉伸压缩，营造强烈不安感。

**English Prompt：**
The camera slowly dollies backward while simultaneously zooming in. The character remains constant in frame size while the valley background stretches and compresses, inducing intense psychological unease.

**核心关键词：** dolly zoom, vertigo effect, perspective distortion, Hitchcock zoom

---

## 焦点平滑转移 (Rack Focus)

引导观众注意力在前景和背景之间切换。

**中文 Prompt：**
镜头保持固定。开始时背景人物清晰，前景人物虚化。随后焦点缓慢转移至前景角色，引导观众关注剧情变化。

**English Prompt：**
The camera remains static. Initially the background character is sharp while the foreground is blurred. Focus then slowly shifts to the foreground character, guiding narrative attention.

**核心关键词：** rack focus, focus pull, attention shift, depth of field transition

---

## 高空俯冲拉升 (Drone Dive and Pull-up)

无人机视角，从高空俯冲到贴地飞行再拉升。

**中文 Prompt：**
镜头从高空鸟瞰开始。无人机快速向下俯冲，贴近河面高速飞行，跟随飞行摩托穿越峡谷。随后镜头迅速拉升至高空，全景展示壮阔山脉与河流地貌，晨雾在阳光照射下缓缓流动。

**English Prompt：**
Starting with a high bird's-eye view, the drone dives rapidly toward a river, flying low at high speed following a hoverbike through a canyon. The camera then quickly pulls up to reveal majestic mountains and flowing morning mist in golden sunlight.

**核心关键词：** aerial shot, drone dive, bird's eye view, epic scale

---

## 第一人称主观视角 (First-Person POV)

角色主观视角，增强沉浸代入感。

**中文 Prompt：**
第一人称视角。镜头模拟角色双眼所看到的画面，随着脚步产生轻微上下起伏。角色穿过废弃实验室，双手偶尔进入画面，警示灯不断闪烁，营造沉浸式探索体验。

**English Prompt：**
First-person POV with slight bobbing motion simulating footsteps. Walking through an abandoned laboratory, hands occasionally enter the frame while warning lights flash continuously, creating an immersive exploration experience.

**核心关键词：** POV shot, first-person, subjective camera, immersive perspective

---

## 无缝一镜到底 (Continuous Long Take / One-shot)

无剪辑连续拍摄，保持时空连贯性。

**中文 Prompt：**
单一连续镜头。摄像机跟随主角穿过走廊、下楼梯、跃过障碍物、穿越大厅并进入电梯。镜头连续完成推进、跟拍和转向动作，全程无任何剪辑切换，营造电影级沉浸感。

**English Prompt：**
A single continuous shot following the protagonist through corridors, down staircases, leaping over obstacles, across a grand lobby, and into an elevator. The camera performs seamless push-ins, tracking, and rotations without any cuts, achieving cinematic immersion.

**核心关键词：** long take, continuous shot, one-shot, seamless choreography

---

## 纪实手持摄影 (Handheld Documentary Style)

自然抖动，增加纪实临场感。

**中文 Prompt：**
手持摄影风格。镜头存在持续轻微抖动和随机运动。记者穿过灾后街区进行采访，周围人群来往穿梭，阳光穿过破损建筑，呈现真实纪录片质感。

**English Prompt：**
Handheld camera style with continuous subtle shake and random motion. A reporter walks through a post-disaster neighborhood conducting interviews, with people flowing past while sunlight pierces through damaged buildings, achieving raw documentary authenticity.

**核心关键词：** handheld camera, natural shake, raw footage, documentary realism

---

## 快速变焦惊吓 (Snap Zoom)

突然快速变焦，制造戏剧冲击。

**中文 Prompt：**
镜头保持中景构图。角色突然看向远处时，镜头快速推进至面部特写，随后迅速恢复原景别。角色表情从平静变为震惊，突出戏剧效果。

**English Prompt：**
Maintaining a medium shot composition, the camera snap-zooms into an extreme close-up when the character suddenly looks into the distance, then quickly snaps back to the original framing. Expression shifts from calm to shock, emphasizing dramatic impact.

**核心关键词：** snap zoom, crash zoom, dramatic emphasis, sudden focus

---

## 过肩对峙观察 (Over-the-Shoulder Tension)

建立对话空间关系，营造紧张氛围。

**中文 Prompt：**
镜头位于主角肩膀后方，保留模糊肩部轮廓。摄像机缓慢移动，对焦于对面的审讯官。房间灯光逐渐变暗，双方眼神对峙，营造紧张氛围。

**English Prompt：**
Positioned behind the protagonist's blurred shoulder silhouette, the camera slowly shifts focus to the interrogator opposite. Room lighting gradually dims as both parties lock eyes, building intense confrontation atmosphere.

**核心关键词：** over-the-shoulder, OTS, dialogue framing, spatial tension

---

## 极速横向拖影 (High-Speed Lateral Track)

高速侧拍，背景形成运动模糊。

**中文 Prompt：**
摄像机从侧面同步跟拍高速冲刺的运动员。主体始终保持在画面中央，背景建筑和灯光形成高速拖影，展现极强速度感和爆发力。

**English Prompt：**
The camera tracks laterally in sync with a sprinting athlete at full speed. The subject remains centered while background architecture and lighting form high-speed motion blur, showcasing explosive velocity and power.

**核心关键词：** lateral tracking, motion blur, speed emphasis, high-velocity

---

## 倾斜镜头 (Dutch Angle / Tilted Frame)

地平线倾斜，表现心理失衡或紧张。

**English Prompt Template：**
[Subject in tense situation], Dutch angle tilted shot with skewed horizon line. The off-balance composition creates psychological tension and unease. [Specific scene detail], [atmospheric lighting description].

**核心关键词：** Dutch angle, tilted camera, skewed horizon, psychological unease

---

## 越轴空间错位 (Crossing the 180-degree Line)

刻意打破空间逻辑，营造迷失感。

**中文 Prompt：**
镜头记录两人面对面对话。画面突然跨越180度空间轴线切换角度，角色站位和朝向似乎发生变化。空间逻辑被刻意打乱，营造迷失感和心理不安感。

**English Prompt：**
Recording a face-to-face dialogue, the camera suddenly crosses the 180-degree spatial axis. Character positions and orientations appear to flip, deliberately breaking spatial logic to evoke disorientation and psychological unease.

**核心关键词：** crossing the line, 180-degree rule break, spatial disorientation
