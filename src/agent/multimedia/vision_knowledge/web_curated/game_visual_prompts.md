## 8 大游戏类型视觉风格锚点

### Cyberpunk / 赛博朋克

**视觉关键词集：**
neon-lit streets, holographic advertisements, rain-soaked pavement, chrome implants, cybernetic augmentations, dark alleyways, Japanese-English mixed signage, flying vehicles, holographic UI overlays, steam and fog, reflective wet surfaces, LED strip lighting

**调色：** teal-and-orange, magenta-cyan contrast, desaturated midtones with vivid neon accents

**光照模板：**
- Key: sodium vapor orange from street lights
- Fill: cold blue-cyan from holographic displays
- Rim: magenta-pink neon from signage
- Atmosphere: volumetric fog + rain particle effects

**Hero Shot Prompt：**
```
A cybernetically enhanced bounty hunter stands on a rain-soaked rooftop overlooking Neo-Tokyo.
Simulated ARRI Alexa + anamorphic lens (40mm f/2.8). Teal-orange grading, heavy film grain.
Neon holographic advertisements reflect off wet chrome augmentations. Camera slowly orbits 180 degrees.
Volumetric fog layers between skyscrapers, distant flying vehicle light trails, storm clouds above.
```

### Fantasy / 奇幻

**视觉关键词集：**
ancient ruins overgrown with vines, floating islands, crystalline magic effects, mythical creatures, enchanted forests, golden hour god rays, mystical runes glowing, cascading waterfalls from floating rocks, medieval armor with magical engravings, ethereal spirit wisps

**调色：** warm amber-gold base with cool forest green accents, high saturation in magic effects

**光照模板：**
- Key: golden hour warm sunlight from upper-right
- Fill: cool forest green bounce from canopy
- Rim: magical aura glow (blue/purple/gold depending on faction)
- Atmosphere: god rays through trees, floating dust particles, magical sparkles

**Hero Shot Prompt：**
```
An ancient elven mage channels golden energy atop crumbling ruins, surrounded by floating stone fragments.
Simulated 65mm IMAX. Warm amber grading with magical blue accents. Rich film grain.
God rays pierce through clouds, illuminating rune-carved pillars. Camera crane-up from medium to epic wide.
Overgrown vines with bioluminescent flowers, distant floating islands in atmospheric haze, mythical dragon silhouette crossing the sun.
```

### Gothic Horror / 哥特恐怖

**视觉关键词集：**
cathedral spires in fog, crumbling Victorian mansions, wrought iron gates, blood moon, bare twisted trees, ravens and crows, candlelit corridors, stained glass windows, gargoyle statues, velvet curtains, cobweb-draped chandeliers, pale moonlight

**调色：** desaturated cool blue-grey base, deep shadows, single warm accent (candle/ember/blood)

**光照模板：**
- Key: cold blue moonlight from high angle
- Fill: minimal, near-black shadows (8:1+ ratio)
- Rim: warm amber candlelight from below
- Atmosphere: dense volumetric fog, dust particles in light beams

**Hero Shot Prompt：**
```
A pale vampire lord descends a grand staircase in a decaying gothic cathedral.
Simulated 35mm film, high-contrast black-and-white with selective red accent. Extreme grain.
Moonlight streams through shattered stained glass, casting colored fragments on marble floors. Camera slow dolly-in.
Candelabra flames flicker in wind, ravens perch on gargoyles, crimson blood drips from a single rose petal.
```

**视觉关键词集：**
rusted vehicles half-buried in sand, collapsed overpasses, makeshift shelters from scrap metal, gas mask silhouettes, dust storms, overgrown urban ruins, barbed wire perimeters, improvised weapons, survivor campfires, cracked asphalt with weeds

**调色：** desaturated warm brown-yellow (Mad Max style), dusty haze, high contrast

**光照模板：**
- Key: harsh overhead sun (bleached, blown-out highlights)
- Fill: warm sand bounce light
- Rim: orange firelight from campfires/explosions
- Atmosphere: dust storm particles, heat haze distortion

**Hero Shot Prompt：**
```
A lone survivor in improvised armor stands atop a rusted highway overpass, surveying endless desert wasteland.
Simulated ARRI Alexa + wide anamorphic (24mm f/8). Bleach-bypass grading, desaturated amber.
Harsh noon sun creates deep shadows under gas mask. Camera slow crane-up revealing vast ruined cityscape.
Abandoned vehicles stretching to horizon, dust storm approaching from the east, a single green plant growing through cracked concrete.
```

### Sci-Fi Space Opera / 太空歌剧

**视觉关键词集：**
capital ships in orbital formation, ringed gas giants, space station interiors, zero-gravity debris, holographic star maps, alien architecture, energy shields, warp drive trails, nebula backgrounds, asteroid fields, crystalline alien technology

**调色：** deep space black + vivid nebula colors (purple-cyan-magenta), cool interior lighting

**光照模板：**
- Key: harsh directional starlight (creating sharp terminator lines)
- Fill: soft reflected light from planetary atmospheres
- Rim: engine glow / shield energy
- Atmosphere: lens flare from stars, particle dust in space

**Hero Shot Prompt：**
```
A massive dreadnought-class warship emerges from hyperspace above a ringed gas giant, fleet formation behind it.
Simulated IMAX 70mm. Deep space black with vivid purple-cyan nebula. Minimal grain.
Harsh starlight creates sharp shadows on hull plating, engine trails still fading from jump. Camera rotating orbital.
Thousands of fighter craft deploy from hangar bays, the planet's rings catching refracted sunlight, a distant supernova remnant glowing.
```

### Stealth / Espionage 潜行谍战

**视觉关键词集：**
shadowy corridors, laser grid security systems, rain on tactical gear, night vision green, suppressed weapon muzzle flash, surveillance camera feeds, high-tech control rooms, rooftop infiltration, zip-line silhouettes, encrypted holographic displays

**调色：** near-monochromatic dark blue-black, single accent color (laser red, NV green, or amber UI)

**光照模板：**
- Key: practical lighting only (security lights, device screens)
- Fill: near-zero, deep shadows conceal
- Rim: laser grid red / night vision green
- Atmosphere: minimal fog, smoke from ventilation

**Hero Shot Prompt：**
```
A tactical operative hangs upside-down from a ventilation shaft, scanning a laser-grid-secured server room below.
Simulated digital cinema camera (50mm f/1.4). Near-monochromatic dark blue with laser red accents.
Only practical security lights create pools of illumination. Camera slow push-in through vent grate.
Rain visible through floor-to-ceiling windows, guard patrol shadows passing, holographic security display reflecting on wet floor.
```

## 游戏预告片通用节奏模板

### Epic Action Trailer (史诗动作)
```
Shot 1: Wide establishing — game world landscape, slow crane, atmospheric (opening)
Shot 2: Medium — hero character introduction, dolly-in, dramatic lighting (build-up)
Shot 3: Action montage — rapid cuts between combat/gameplay moments (climax)
Shot 4: Hero pose — slow motion, energy effects, title card transition (resolution)
```

### Horror Trailer (恐怖)
```
Shot 1: Dark corridor, slow push-in, practical light flickering (opening)
Shot 2: Close-up — character's fearful reaction, rack focus to background shadow (build-up)
Shot 3: Jump scare — snap zoom + dolly zoom, flash frame (climax)
Shot 4: Silence — wide empty shot, single moving element, fade to black (resolution)
```

### Emotional Drama Trailer (情感剧情)
```
Shot 1: Wide landscape — character small in frame, golden hour (opening)
Shot 2: Intimate close-up — emotional moment, shallow DOF (build-up)
Shot 3: Montage — relationship moments, match cuts, rising intensity (climax)
Shot 4: Pull-out reveal — context changes meaning of earlier shots, title (resolution)
```

### Esports / Competitive Trailer (电竞)
```
Shot 1: Arena establishing — crowd energy, neon stage lighting (opening)
Shot 2: Player close-up — focus and determination, screen light on face (build-up)
Shot 3: Gameplay montage — ultra-fast cuts, kill streaks, clutch moments (climax)
Shot 4: Victory moment — slow motion celebration, confetti, logo reveal (resolution)
```
